# Client onboarding

Collect everything needed to build and write the website. Keep it light: four short rounds, a few questions each. Do not interrogate. If an answer already covers a later question, skip that question.

If `client/profile.md` already exists, show a one-paragraph summary of it and ask whether to update it or start fresh before asking anything.

## Round 1 — The business

Ask conversationally, two or three questions at a time:

1. What is the business? (name + what it does, in their own words)
2. Where does it operate, and who is the ideal customer?
3. What is the one action a visitor should take on the site? (call, WhatsApp, book, buy, fill a form)

## Round 2 — The money keyword

1. What is the money keyword — the single search term that, if you ranked #1 for it, would bring in paying customers? Ask for exactly one primary keyword.
2. Any supporting keywords they also care about (optional, up to 4).
3. Who are the two or three competitors that currently rank for it (optional)?

If they give a vague keyword ("marketing"), help them sharpen it into something a real buyer types ("performance marketing agency in Chennai"). Suggest, don't decide for them.

## Round 3 — EEAT proof

Explain in one sentence why you're asking: search engines and AI answers favor pages with demonstrated experience and trust. Then collect only what is TRUE:

1. Who is the face of the business? (founder/owner name, role, one-line bio)
2. Years in business, credentials, certifications, licenses
3. Achievements: awards, press mentions, notable clients, speaking, publications
4. Numbers they can stand behind: clients served, projects delivered, results with figures
5. Two or three real testimonials or review snippets they want featured (with the reviewer's first name and source)

Record answers exactly as given. Never round up, embellish, or fill gaps with invented proof. If they have nothing for a question, leave that section out of the site — an empty section is fine, a fake one is not.

## Round 4 — Reference screenshots

1. Ask them to paste screenshots of the website(s) they want theirs to look like, or drop the image files into `client/references/`. One screenshot per section is ideal (hero, services, pricing, testimonials, footer).
2. For each screenshot ask: what specifically do you like here — the layout, the colors, the vibe, a particular section?
3. If they name a live site instead, note the URL and which pages/sections to model.

Save any images pasted in chat to `client/references/` with descriptive names (`hero-reference.png`, `pricing-reference.png`).

## Finish

1. Write `client/profile.md` using `profile-template.md` in this skill folder.
2. Show a compact summary (5–8 lines) and ask them to confirm or correct it.
3. On confirmation, tell them the next step is `/build` and stop. Do not start building unless they ask.
