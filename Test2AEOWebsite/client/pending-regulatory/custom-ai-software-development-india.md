## 3. Section 8 and DPDP Rules paragraph
- Where: under H2 "How should an AI vendor handle your data under the DPDP Act?"
- Original text: "Section 8 of the Act says a Data Fiduciary remains responsible for processing done on its behalf by a Data Processor, and may engage one only under a valid contract. The same section requires reasonable security safeguards to prevent a personal data breach, notice of any breach to the Data Protection Board and to each affected person, and erasure of data by the processor once the purpose is served, unless the law requires retention. The DPDP Rules, 2025 were notified in November 2025 with an 18-month phased compliance timeline, according to the Press Information Bureau. Some obligations are still phasing in, so confirm current dates with your legal adviser."
- Relied on: https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf ; https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/22037?locale=en ; https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190014

- Where: "Frequently asked questions" section, and FAQPage JSON-LD
- Original question: "Does the DPDP Act apply when we hire an AI development company?"
