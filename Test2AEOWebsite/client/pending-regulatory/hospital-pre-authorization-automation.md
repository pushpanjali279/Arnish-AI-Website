## 3. Section H2 "What timelines does IRDAI set for cashless authorization and discharge?" (id="irdai-timelines")
- Where: full H2 section between "How does a cashless claim move from admission to discharge?" and "Where do pre-authorization delays and queries come from?"
- Source relied on: IRDAI, Master Circular on Health Insurance Business, 29 May 2024 (Ref. IRDAI/HLT/CIR/PRO/84/5/2024), https://irdai.gov.in/document-detail?documentId=4942918
- Original text:

> What timelines does IRDAI set for cashless authorization and discharge?

> IRDAI's Master Circular on Health Insurance Business, issued on 29 May 2024, asks insurers to decide on a cashless authorization request immediately, and within one hour of receiving it. Final authorization at discharge is due within three hours of the insurer receiving the hospital's discharge authorization request.

> The circular also says a policyholder should not be made to wait for discharge. If the three-hour limit is crossed, any additional amount the hospital charges is to be borne by the insurer from its shareholders' fund. Insurers are asked to work towards 100 percent cashless settlement in a time-bound manner, and insurers and TPAs are to collect the required documents from the hospital, not from the policyholder.

> These clocks run on the insurer's side, and they start when the insurer receives the request. Time a hospital spends assembling a file, or the gap between the doctor signing off and the discharge file going out, falls outside them, and incomplete files invite queries. Sending complete files promptly is the hospital's share of the work, and it is the part automation can help with.

> IRDAI reviews its master circulars periodically, so check the current text on the IRDAI website before you build rules around these figures.

## 6. FAQ "Is NHCX mandatory for hospitals?" (visible FAQ and FAQPage JSON-LD)
- Where: Frequently asked questions, fourth item
- Source relied on: https://irdai.gov.in/document-detail?documentId=4942918
- Original text:

> Q: Is NHCX mandatory for hospitals?
> A: The 2024 Master Circular says insurers, with the Insurance Councils, may endeavour to onboard hospitals on the National Health Claims Exchange. That wording encourages adoption rather than requiring it. Check current IRDAI and National Health Authority notices, and ask your empanelled insurers and TPAs which of them accept requests through NHCX.
