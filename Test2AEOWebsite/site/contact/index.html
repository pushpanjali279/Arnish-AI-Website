FAQ

Questions about booking a consultation

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The first consultation has no charge and no obligation. We use it to understand your process and tell you whether and how software can help.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Describe the process you want to improve, the systems you use today and roughly how many claims, patients or transactions it handles. A short description is enough, and we will ask follow-up questions.

Can we meet in person?

Our registered office is in Shirpur, Dhule district, Maharashtra. Most consultations happen online because we work with clients across India and abroad, and you can ask about an in-person meeting in your enquiry.

What happens to the details I send?

We use them only to reply to your enquiry and prepare for the consultation. Please do not include patient records or policyholder personal data in the form.
