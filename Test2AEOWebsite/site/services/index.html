Insurance automation solutions and AI software services

Arnish AI, a software company in Shirpur, Maharashtra, builds insurance automation solutions for health insurers, TPAs and hospitals, along with AI applications, chatbots, custom software and analytics. Claim automation is our main service. Each project is scoped in a free consultation and built around the process your team already runs.

Main focus

What does your insurance claim automation service include?

We build software that prepares health insurance claims for decision. It collects the documents, reads them, extracts the data, checks it against the policy, flags gaps and sends the file to the right person. It covers both cashless and reimbursement claims.

For a TPA this usually means one intake queue across several insurer clients, each with its own rules. For an insurer it often means fewer files bouncing back to processors because a discharge summary or bill was missing.

Read our guide: how insurance claim processing automation works in India.

Included in a claim automation build

  • Intake from email, portals and scanned paper
  • Text extraction from bills, prescriptions, reports and discharge summaries
  • Policy rule checks: sum insured, sub-limits, co-payment, waiting periods, exclusions
  • Missing-document and duplicate-claim flags
  • Routing to processors and medical reviewers, with a full audit log
  • Status updates for hospitals and policyholders

How does your hospital automation software help insurance desks?

Our hospital software reduces the back-and-forth on cashless claims. It keeps a document checklist for each insurer and TPA, fills request forms from data the hospital already holds, and tracks every pending query so nothing waits unnoticed before discharge.

We also build admission, billing and reporting tools for hospital administration. Read more in our guide to hospital pre-authorization automation.

Typical hospital features

  • Pre-authorization request preparation and tracking
  • Per-insurer document checklists
  • Query and enhancement tracking with alerts
  • Discharge and final bill approval follow-up
  • Reports on pending, approved and rejected claims

What AI applications and chatbots do you build?

We build chatbots that answer customer and staff questions from your own documents and data, such as policy coverage or claim status. We also add AI features to existing software, including document reading, classification, summarisation and search.

Every AI feature we build has a plan for when it is wrong: confidence thresholds, a route to a person, and logs your team can check.

Examples

  • Claim-status and policy question chatbots
  • Document classification and data extraction
  • Summaries of long medical or case files for reviewers
  • Search across internal documents

What data analytics and BI services do you offer?

We build dashboards and reports from your operational data so managers can see what is happening without compiling spreadsheets by hand. For claims teams, that usually means turnaround time, rejection and query reasons, pending files and workload per processor.

Common reports

  • Claim turnaround time by stage
  • Rejection and query reasons
  • Pending files and ageing
  • Workload per team and per processor

FAQ

Questions about our services

How much does insurance automation software cost?

The cost depends on how many claim types, document formats and systems the software has to handle. After a free consultation we write down the scope and give you an estimate for that scope before any work starts.

Can your software connect to our existing claim or hospital system?

In most cases, yes. We connect through the system's API, database exports or file drops, depending on what your current system allows. We confirm the connection method during scoping, before we commit to a plan.

How long does a claim automation project take?

It depends on scope. A first version that covers one claim type is much smaller than a full platform for several insurers. We give you a timeline in the written scope and show working software at each checkpoint.

Do you work with startups and non-healthcare businesses?

Yes. Healthcare and insurance are our main focus, and we also build custom software, SaaS products, chatbots and dashboards for startups, SMEs and enterprises in other sectors.
