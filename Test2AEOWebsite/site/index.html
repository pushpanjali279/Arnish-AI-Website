Claim automation

What does insurance claim processing automation software do?

It takes over the clerical steps of a health claim. The software reads scanned bills and discharge summaries, pulls out the fields a processor would type by hand, compares them with the policy and flags anything missing or out of limit. A person then reviews a prepared file instead of a pile of paper.

We build this around how your team already works. If your processors check room rent limits first and your doctors review diagnosis codes second, the software follows that order and shows each person only what they need to decide.

How we build claim automation

Steps we automate in a claim

  • Document intake from email, portal uploads and scans
  • Text extraction from bills, prescriptions and discharge summaries
  • Policy checks for sum insured, sub-limits, waiting periods and exclusions
  • Flags for missing documents and duplicate claims
  • Routing to the right processor or medical reviewer
  • Status updates for hospitals and policyholders

FAQ

Questions about insurance claim processing automation

What is insurance claim processing automation software?

It is software that handles the repetitive parts of a health insurance claim: reading documents, extracting data, checking it against policy terms and routing the file to a reviewer. People still decide the claims that need judgement. The software removes the typing and the hunting for missing papers.

Who does Arnish AI build software for?

Arnish AI works with health insurance companies, Third-Party Administrators (TPAs), hospitals, insurance agencies, and startups and enterprises that want custom software or AI added to their operations. Our main focus is healthcare and insurance.

Does Arnish AI only work with clients in Shirpur?

No. Arnish AI is headquartered in Shirpur, Maharashtra, and works with clients across India and in other countries. Consultations, project reviews and support happen online.

Can automation replace claim reviewers?

We do not design it to. Automation handles data entry, document checks and routing, and reviewers keep the final decision on claims that need medical or policy judgement. Reviewers then spend their time on the cases that need a person.
