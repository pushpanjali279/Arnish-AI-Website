What we do

What kind of company is Arnish AI?

We are a software company that builds AI-powered applications, automation and custom platforms. Our main customers are health insurers, Third-Party Administrators, hospitals and insurance agencies, and we also build for startups, SMEs and enterprises in other sectors.

Our background

Why does Arnish AI focus on healthcare and insurance?

Arnish AI is a subsidiary of Bullet Healthcare, and our software work concentrates on healthcare and health insurance. We focus on claims, pre-authorization and hospital administration because the processes are document-heavy, rule-bound and repeated many times a day.

Rules software can check

Sum insured, sub-limits, co-payments, waiting periods and required documents follow written rules. Software can check them the same way on every file.

Decisions people should keep

Medical necessity and disputed claims need a qualified person. We design systems so that person sees a clean, complete file and makes the call.

How we work

How does Arnish AI approach a software project?

These are the working rules we apply to every project, whether it is claim automation or a custom web application.

  1. Process before code

    We map the current workflow with the people who run it before we write any software.

  2. Test on real files

    We test extraction and rules on your own documents, because sample data hides the messy cases.

  3. Humans decide

    Automation prepares and routes work. Judgement calls stay with your team, and every action is logged.

  4. Data access is limited

    Health and policy data is shared only with the people and systems that need it, and access is logged.
